Mental Health Tips

Practice Self-Care

Caring for yourself helps you care for others too.

Self-care looks different for everyone

Body
Eat regular meals, drink water, move in ways you enjoy and rest when you need to.
Mind
Take breaks from the news, try a new hobby or spend time on something that interests you.
Feelings
Name how you feel, write about it, and be patient with yourself on hard days.
Connection
Spend time with people who support you, or reach out to someone you have not spoken to in a while.

Start small

  • Step outside for five minutes of fresh air.
  • Do one thing each day just because you enjoy it.
  • Say no to something that you do not have the energy for.
  • End the day by noting one thing that went well.

You don’t have to do it alone

Sharing with others can make self-care easier to keep up. Explore support groups.
