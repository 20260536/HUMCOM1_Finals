About Us

A bridge to the support you need.

MindBridge helps people find trusted mental health resources, professionals and communities in one calm, easy-to-use place.

Our mission

Reaching out can be the hardest step. We want it to be a little easier, by gathering clear information and trusted support in one place so that no one has to face it alone.

We share practical tips, list verified and licensed professionals, and connect people with supportive groups.

What we value

  • Trusted

    We focus on verified and licensed professionals and reliable information.

  • Practical

    Our guides give simple steps you can try right away.
