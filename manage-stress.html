Mental Health Tips

Manage Stress

Stress is a normal part of life, but it does not have to take over.

Notice the signs

Stress shows up differently for everyone. Common signs include:

  • Tight shoulders, headaches or an upset stomach.
  • Trouble concentrating or making decisions.
  • Feeling irritable, restless or overwhelmed.
  • Sleeping much more or much less than usual.

Five things to try

  1. Breathe slowly. Breathe in for four counts, then out for six. Repeat for a few minutes.
  2. Write it down. List what is worrying you, then pick one small task to do first.
  3. Move your body. A short walk or some gentle stretching can ease tension.
  4. Take real breaks. Step away from screens and work for a few minutes each hour.
  5. Talk to someone. Sharing how you feel with a trusted person can lighten the load.

When stress keeps building

If stress stays high for weeks or affects your daily life, it may help to talk with someone. Find a professional or join a support group.
