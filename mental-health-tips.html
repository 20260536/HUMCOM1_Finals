Mental Health Tips

Small Steps. Big Differences.

Learn simple ways to care for your mental health, every day.

Choose a topic

  • Manage Stress

    Stress is a normal part of life, but it does not have to take over.

    • Take slow, deep breaths for a few minutes.
    • Write down what is worrying you and break it into small tasks.
    • Take short breaks and move your body.

    Read more about managing stress

  • Get Better Sleep

    Good sleep supports your mood, focus and energy.

    • Keep a regular sleep and wake time.
    • Avoid screens and caffeine before bed.
    • Keep your room dark, quiet and comfortable.

    Read more about better sleep

  • Practice Self-Care

    Caring for yourself helps you care for others too.

    • Do something you enjoy each day.
    • Eat regular meals and drink enough water.
    • Spend time with people who support you.

    Read more about self-care

More about each topic

Manage Stress

Notice what triggers your stress and how your body reacts. Simple habits such as breathing exercises, short walks and talking with someone you trust can lower tension over time.

Get Better Sleep

Create a calming bedtime routine, such as reading or gentle stretching. If sleep problems last for weeks, consider speaking with a health professional.

Practice Self-Care

Self-care looks different for everyone. It can be resting, setting boundaries, spending time outdoors or doing a hobby. Start small and be kind to yourself.
