Your privacy matters

This policy explains what information MindBridge collects and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us, such as:

  • Your name, email address and message when you use the contact form.
  • Search terms and filter choices you enter on the site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and feedback.
  • To improve the site and the resources we share.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only if the law requires it or if you ask us to.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not currently use cookies or tracking scripts.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time.

Contact us

For privacy questions, please contact us.
