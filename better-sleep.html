Mental Health Tips

Get Better Sleep

Good sleep supports your mood, focus and energy.

Habits that help

  • Go to bed and wake up at about the same time each day.
  • Keep your bedroom dark, quiet and comfortable.
  • Get some daylight and movement during the day.
  • Avoid caffeine and large meals late in the evening.
  • Put screens away for a while before bed.

A simple bedtime routine

  1. Pick a time to start winding down, about an hour before bed.
  2. Dim the lights and put your phone out of reach.
  3. Do something calming, such as reading, gentle stretching or a warm shower.
  4. If your mind is busy, write down tomorrow’s tasks so you can let them go.

If you can’t fall asleep

Try not to watch the clock. If you are still awake after a while, get up and do something quiet in low light until you feel sleepy, then return to bed.

When sleep problems continue

If trouble sleeping lasts for weeks or affects your mood, health or daily life, consider speaking with a health professional. Browse professionals.
