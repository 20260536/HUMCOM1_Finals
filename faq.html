Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using MindBridge.

Using MindBridge

What is MindBridge?

MindBridge is a website that helps you find mental health tips, professionals and support groups in one place.

Is MindBridge a replacement for therapy?

No. MindBridge shares general information and resources. It does not give diagnosis or treatment. Please speak with a licensed professional about your own situation.

Does it cost anything to use the site?

Browsing the site is free. Professionals and groups set their own fees, so please check with them directly.

Professionals

How do I choose a professional?

Think about what you want help with, check that they are licensed, and ask about fees and availability. Our Professionals page has a short guide.

What is the difference between a psychiatrist and a psychologist?

A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who can prescribe medication. A psychologist usually provides assessment and talk therapy.

Can I change professionals if it doesn’t feel right?

Yes. Finding the right fit matters, and it is fine to try someone else.

Support groups

Do I have to speak in a group?

No. You can listen first and share only when you feel ready.

Are groups private?

Most groups ask members to keep what is shared confidential. Ask the facilitator about the group’s rules before you join.

Where can I find upcoming sessions?

See our Events and Workshops page.

Still have a question?

Contact us and we will get back to you.

MindBridge brings trusted mental health resources, professionals and communities together, so you don’t have to face it alone.
