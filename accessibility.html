Accessibility Statement

MindBridge should be easy to use for everyone, whatever device or assistive technology you use.

Our commitment

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We keep improving the site as we learn.

Accessibility features

  • A skip link at the top of every page that jumps to the main content.
  • Clear page titles, headings and landmarks for screen reader navigation.
  • Full keyboard access with a visible focus outline.
  • Text descriptions for meaningful images.
  • Labels on every form field.
  • Strong colour contrast between text and background.
  • Layouts that adapt to small screens and larger text sizes.
  • No JavaScript is needed to use the site.

Tips for adjusting the site

You can zoom in with your browser (Ctrl or Cmd and the plus key) or change the default text size in your browser settings.

Tell us about a problem

If you find something hard to use, please contact us and choose “Accessibility issue” as the topic. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will do our best to fix it.
