Why join a support group?

  • Share your experiences in a safe, understanding space.
  • Learn coping strategies from others.
  • Feel less isolated.
  • Build lasting connections.

What to expect

Most groups meet regularly and are led by a trained facilitator or peer. You can listen at first and share only when you are ready. What is said in the group stays in the group.
