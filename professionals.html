Types of professionals

Psychiatrist
A medical doctor who can diagnose mental health conditions and prescribe medication.
Psychologist
Provides assessment and talk therapy for a wide range of concerns.
Counsellor
Offers guidance and support for stress, relationships, grief and life changes.
Social worker
Helps connect people with therapy, community services and practical support.

Choosing the right professional

  1. Check that the professional is licensed and verified.
  2. Think about what you want help with.
  3. Ask about fees, availability and whether sessions are in person or online.
  4. Choose someone you feel comfortable talking to. It is fine to try more than one.

Prefer to connect with peers?

Many people find it helpful to share experiences with others. Explore support groups.
